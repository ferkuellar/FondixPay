# Deprovisioning Policy

## Current State

No production users, providers, or payment credentials are active in Phase 1.

## Future Policy Areas

- User account deactivation.
- Session revocation.
- Admin/support access removal.
- Provider credential rotation and removal.
- Device/session cleanup.
- Audit retention after deactivation.
- Data deletion or anonymization when legally allowed.

## Financial Constraint

Financial records, receipts, ledger entries, and audit events may require retention even if user access is removed. Exact retention rules require legal/compliance review.
