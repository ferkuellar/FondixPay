# Hotfix Card-Only Payment Strategy Requirements

## Goal
Correct roadmap and documentation so FondixPay is card-only for user-facing service payments.

## Required Corrections
- Debit/credit card is the only user-facing payment method in the current roadmap.
- Prontipagos remains the service-payment aggregator, separate from the future card processor.
- Mock UX references card demo.
- No SPEI, CoDi, OXXO/store payment, cash-in, cash, bank transfer, wallet balance, or stored balance is presented as a user-facing payment method.
