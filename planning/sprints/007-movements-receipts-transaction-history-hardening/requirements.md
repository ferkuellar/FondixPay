# Phase 7 Requirements

## Goal
Harden mock/dev transaction history and receipt surfaces so payment status, receipt status, fee breakdown, method label, mock references, and next action remain clear.

## Included
- Mobile history filters for succeeded, pending, and failed mock attempts.
- Receipt detail surface with payment and receipt status.
- Recovery attempts recorded in local mobile history.
- Mock/dev disclaimers on receipt/history surfaces.
- AXON-AI docs, risks, decisions, and completion evidence.

## Excluded
- Real provider confirmation.
- Prontipagos integration.
- Real receipt download/share.
- Real reconciliation, reversals, support console, or money movement.
