# Phase 6A — Account & Balance Model Design Requirements

## Goal
Define the product account and balance model before any simulated balance or real wallet implementation.

## In Scope
- Account concepts and statuses.
- Available, pending, held, simulated, and future real balance semantics.
- Ledger-derived movement model.
- Future account/balance APIs.
- Audit, security, UX, validation, operations, roadmap, and backlog updates.

## Out of Scope
- Wallet real.
- Real balance.
- Funding, withdrawal, SPEI, CoDi, OXXO, banks, or Prontipagos.
- Mobile UI.
- Backend models/migrations/endpoints.
- KYC and regulated-account claims.

## Acceptance
Account/balance semantics are documented and Phase 6B can implement only demo/simulated balance under explicit labels.
