# Adoption Map

## Current Adoption Target

Internal product and engineering validation only.

## Future Adoption Stages

1. Internal development validation.
2. Internal stakeholder demo with mock/dev disclaimer.
3. Closed QA with non-real payment flows.
4. Sandbox provider pilot after provider selection.
5. Limited production pilot only after security, audit, ledger, compliance, and operations gates.
6. Public mobile release after store readiness and production release approval.

## Non-Negotiable Gate

No real users should be asked to move real money until the product passes ledger, audit, provider, security, validation, and operations gates.
