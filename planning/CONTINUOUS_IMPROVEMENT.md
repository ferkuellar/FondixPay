# Continuous Improvement

## Cadence

- Review risks at the end of every phase.
- Update `planning/STATE.md` after meaningful changes.
- Record durable decisions in `planning/DECISIONS.md`.
- Move unresolved scope into `planning/QUESTIONS.md`.
- Keep sprint handoffs file-based.

## Feedback Sources

- QA findings.
- Support cases.
- Security reviews.
- Incident reports.
- Payment/provider reconciliation issues.
- Mobile analytics and crash reports when implemented.

## Rule

Improvements must be routed through roadmap phases or approved sprint scope. Avoid adding product behavior opportunistically during hardening or documentation phases.
